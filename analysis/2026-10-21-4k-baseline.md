Coefficient of variation across the final three runs:

- total throughput: 1.82%;
- mean TTFT: 0.43%;
- mean TPOT: 1.15%.

Compared with the public TianShu page reference:

- local total throughput is 7.5% lower than `2028.01 tok/s`;
- local mean TTFT is 6.4% higher than `11573.53 ms`;
- the visible 32 GiB accelerator instance is not assumed to be equivalent to
  the page's scoring hardware state.

## Latency interpretation

Using `Mean TTFT + (output_len - 1) * Mean TPOT` as a diagnostic proxy:

- estimated mean request latency is 172.66 s;
- estimated TTFT share is 7.13%;
- estimated decode share is 92.87%.

TTFT includes scheduling and queuing. The estimate is not a GPU timeline, but
it shows that 4K optimization must address decode as well as prefill.
